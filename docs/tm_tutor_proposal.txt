TM / TUTOR PROPOSAL: deliver physical coverage (2026-11-22)
===========================================================
Goal: instead of trimming trainer sets to fit thin movepools, give mons
access to the right-category coverage moves through TMs and the Viridian
drink tutor. Physical coverage is the thin side: these moves are level-up
only today: Rock Slide, Crunch, Shadow Claw, X-Scissor, Poison Jab,
Icicle Crash, Seed Bomb, Dragon Claw.

Budget: the per-species TM/HM/tutor bitfield is (50 + 8 + 17 + 7) / 8 =
10 bytes = 80 bits, 75 used. Up to 5 more tutors are free; past 80 every
species grows by a byte. TM swaps cost nothing.


1. TM SWAPS (same TM number, same location, new move)
-----------------------------------------------------

TM40  DEFENSE_CURL   96       9        Mt Mortar 2F       ROCK_SLIDE (75)

- Order of access: Shadow Claw before Violet (same power as Headbutt),
  Rock Slide mid-game in Mt Mortar, Crunch at the Route 39 farmhouse.
- DETECT stays: it is a player-facing item-detect move now (another
  session restored its TM, 80feb7fe6).
- Defense Curl loses its TM, but the Rollout users that care (Miltank,
  Donphan line, Geodude line) learn it by level-up anyway.
- Route 31 and the Route 39 farmhouse NPC text may name the old moves;
  check and rewrite (18-char rule).

Kept on purpose: SWEET_SCENT (field move), FURY_CUTTER (Bugsy's theme),
MUD_SLAP (Falkner's), HIDDEN_POWER, ENDURE, SLEEP_TALK.
Spare TMs if more is wanted later: TM21
PAIN_SPLIT (2), TM09 PSYCH_UP (11). Candidates: DRAGON_CLAW, or
DARK_PULSE (special Dark is also level-up only).

Ice Beam); granting those to Alakazam/Ampharos is a pure compat edit.
