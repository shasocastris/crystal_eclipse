parties: special coverage for Alakazam, Ampharos, Jolteon and friends

- Alakazam (50 Atk) drops most of its elemental punches. The first
  punch becomes Shadow Ball and a second one becomes Zap Cannon.
  Sabrina's two keep Fire Punch.
- Ampharos: Thunderpunch -> Thunderbolt. The physical Fire Punch / Body
  Slam / Strength stay: there's no special equivalent.
- Blue (2) Gengar had no STAB: Shadow Ball and Hypnosis in place of the
  punches. Only one of Ghost/Dark, since they hit almost the same
  things.
- Jolteon: Double Kick / Pin Missile / Bite become Shadow Ball, Thunder
  Wave or Double Team.
- Rival1 Haunter: Lick -> Night Shade at Lv25, Hypnosis at Lv50 (it
  already has Shadow Ball).
- Flareon: Shadow Ball -> Iron Tail (Blaine 1, Will 2, Tin Tower Sage).
- Route 43 Butterfree: Headbutt -> Gust (Lv32/38) or Giga Drain
  (Lv47/52).
