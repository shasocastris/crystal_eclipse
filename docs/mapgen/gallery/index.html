Recommended workflow

  • Idea and layout: I write the concept and a text-grid draft (or seed one from the layout-first generator), you react to the render, we iterate.
  • Polish: open the .ablk in Polished Map++, which is already installed (/usr/local/bin/polishedmap-plusplus, 2.7.1). It reads this repo's per-block attributes correctly. Plain Polished Map 4.7.1 is also installed but drops the attributes, so don't use it here.
  • Seasons and time of day: Polished Map++ can't show them; preview with render_ablk.py --season winter --time nite.
  • Wire into the build: newmap.py prints every snippet for the ten files a new map touches. The checklist is further down.

Expanded Johto · New Bark to Violet City and its routes

The goal behind all of this: Johto's own maps, still recognisable but larger and with more to explore. Pokefollow's expanded Kanto was the model. Sources and the full analysis are in docs/mapgen/expand/.

What pokefollow does

  • Keeps the landmarks and their layout. Pallet goes from 10×9 to 24×20, but Red's and Blue's houses are still side by side with Oak's Lab south of them.
  • Scale depends on the map type. Tiny towns grow about 2.4× per side, routes double their width, and Viridian (1.3×) and Pewter (1.0×) get denser rather than larger.
  • Water and bridges split towns into neighbourhoods, and every plot gets a fence, hedge or flower-bed edge.
  • Ragged, organic edges replace straight walls. Side areas are separate small maps (Pallet Lake, Oak Corral).

All eight pass the walkability check. The real cost of implementing them is re-pointing every warp, scene trigger, Fly spot and map connection.

Batch 2 · twelve outdoor maps and two mansions

Six more designers, all working in text grids. Every map below passes the walkability check: exits and doors connect on foot, and anything behind Cut, Surf, Strength or Rock Smash is gated on purpose. Each .amap carries its own design notes. Sources are in docs/mapgen/batch2/.

How they could fit together

  • Mt. Mortar country: Route 42 → Hearthhollow → Driftbank Ford (south) → Pinefall Lake. Mortar Foothills runs east from Hearthhollow to Mahogany, and Starfall Crater sits up in the high ground.
  • The orchard belt: Orchard Road → Reedmere Marsh → Silverbough Estate, whose manor is either Oborozuki Manor or Vesper Hall.
  • A new coast: Mulberry Gardens → Silkwater City → Brinehaven, the ferry port to the Orange Islands. Tidewatch Cape hangs off Olivine or Route 40.
  • Near Violet: Sunleaf Terraces north of Route 36; Stoneway off Route 32 toward the Ruins of Alph.

Batch 3 · six Goldenrod-style cities

Six large cities on the Johto Modern tileset, each built from Goldenrod's own building footprints: dense street grids, districts and a landmark. None has a Pokémon Center. Each has an inn or hotel with a PC instead. Every door and edge exit is reachable on foot, except for two doors a guard blocks until the story moves on.

Tileset gaps all six agents ran into

  • One office front: block 5f is hard-labelled "PCC" and turns up on Link Centers, galleries and shops. It needs a plain variant.
  • One grey tower: the same Radio Tower art stands in for a TV mast, a furnace stack, an observatory, a clock tower and a signal box.
  • No street furniture: no lamps, benches, statues, crates or real fountains. Fountains are fenced ponds.
  • Water and rails: no bottom shore, bridge deck or sand. Tracks run west–east only, with no corners or buffer stops.
  • Unused blocks to check: the pillared front 7c/7d and the brown roof middle 7b never appear in Goldenrod. Check their attributes in Polished Map++.

1 · Hand-designed concepts

The design agent studied Routes 29–42 and the Johto towns first. Its notes on the house style: tree walls 1–3 blocks thick, a 1-wide path turning every 3–6 blocks, ledges as optional one-way shortcuts, tall grass in small pockets beside the path, and about half of every route as forest padding.

Maps are written as text grids (.amap) with a per-map key, so the file looks like the map. amap.py writes the .ablk, renders it with trainer, item and sign markers, and runs the walkability check.

Suggested placement

Route 42 already has a fruit-tree alcove touching its southern edge. Moving its three apricorn trees turns it into Hearthhollow's north exit, giving Route 42 → Hearthhollow → Orchard Road → Route 37, linked by apricorns.

Known rough edges

  • The tileset has no bottom-shore block, so ponds meet trees or paths directly. Vanilla does the same.
  • Watch for Cut gates. Block 5b blocks the way correctly, but 63 placed sideways blocks nothing.

2 · Layout-first generator

structgen.py works like a level designer. It places exits, winds a main path between alternating waypoints, then adds dead-end branches with items, a Cut pocket, ledge bands, ponds or a lake with a Surf island, tall grass, signs and trainer spots. An autotiler then picks real block IDs, using rules mined from all 34 Johto maps: tree corners from collision quadrants, pond shores, ledges, Cut gates and buildings.

Every map is checked for exits reachable both ways, reachable doors, one-way ledges and softlocks. About 8 seeds per preset came out clean.

3 · Caves

The Johto tileset has no cave blocks, so this agent used TILESET_CAVE, the generic cave tileset shared by Union Cave, Mt. Moon and Victory Road. A cave has two heights: dark low floor and light high rock. All the edge blocks follow one rule mined from the 18 vanilla caves, and rock must be at least two blocks thick. Darkness is a per-map flag, so any of these could require Flash.

Not generated, but natural next steps: Strength boulder puzzles over block 3f holes, Rock Smash rocks capping dead ends, a dark B1F as an early Flash hook.

4 · Pattern learning (WFC)

Wave function collapse learns which blocks can sit next to each other from 15 inland routes, then fills a grid. Single-block adjacency produced soup. 3×3 patterns copied training maps nearly verbatim. 2×2 patterns, with a tree border and a carved road, were the best of the variants.

The agent rated those "first drafts". Looking at them, I disagree: roads spread into wide blobs, cliff plateaus float in the forest, and buildings appear with no path to their doors. Cleaning one up is more work than starting from the layout-first generator. Towns failed outright.

Adding a map to the build

From the commits that added Valencia and Shamouti. newmap.py prints each snippet with its insert line and never writes to the repo.

  • constants/map_constants.asm: append to the end of a group only. Limit is (W+6)×(H+6) ≤ 1300.
  • data/maps/maps.asm: header row. Environment TOWN or ROUTE, since it also drives seasons, roofs and weather.
  • data/maps/attributes.asm: connections in N, S, W, E order, each mirrored on the neighbour. That bank has 1 byte free but floats.
  • data/maps/blocks.asm: "Map Blocks 3" (bank $2c) has 5,876 bytes free.
  • data/maps/scripts.asm: "Map Scripts 29" has 2 bytes left, so start "Map Scripts 30".
  • Scenes: a missing scene_var fails silently, and scene bytes can't be appended. Reuse a dead one.
  • Landmarks: reuse an existing one if you can. A landmark added at the end counts as Orange in the region rules.
  • Wild data: bank $0a has 10 bytes free and is fragile. The bank $75 seasonal tables are the safer route, still unverified.
  • New map group: roofs, roof palettes (both), and MapGroupSizes. All are assert-checked.
  • Event flags: 29 free const_skip slots remain.
