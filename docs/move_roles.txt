TAKEAWAYS
---------

- Real gaps the cut would open: Fairy special (Moonblast), Steel special
  (Flash Cannon), physical Grass for Leafeon/Tangrowth (Seed Bomb).
- Weakest moves on the keep list: ZEN_HEADBUTT, WILD_CHARGE, DRAGON_PULSE
  each have one real user or none. Drop them too, or hand them out wider.
- Strongest new additions: FLARE_BLITZ and ICICLE_SPEAR. FAIRY_WIND is
  redundant with DISARM_VOICE.
- Cutting renumbers moves and breaks saves (moves are stored by index).
